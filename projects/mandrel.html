Hot Strip Mill Mandrel

CAD design and structural analysis for steel production equipment—optimizing performance under extreme operating conditions.

Category CAD & Analysis
Application Industrial Equipment
Software SolidWorks · FEA

Requirements

Design for high temperature, rotational speed, and mechanical loads in steel rolling operations.

Analysis

Finite element analysis to evaluate stress, thermal expansion, and fatigue life.

Optimization

Iterative refinement balancing performance with manufacturability.

Modeling

3D CAD Development

Parametric modeling in SolidWorks captured dimensional requirements, interface geometry, and material specifications. Design constraints included rotational balance and thermal expansion accommodation.

Analysis

FEA & Optimization

Finite element analysis evaluated stress concentrations under operating loads, thermal gradients, and fatigue cycles. Results guided geometry refinement to optimize strength-to-weight ratio.

Final Design

Optimized Geometry

The final design met all performance requirements while maintaining manufacturability, with documented analysis supporting safety factors and expected service life.
