Carson Custom Guitars

Custom electric and bass guitar design and manufacturing—combining traditional woodworking with modern design.

Category Woodworking
Type Electric & Bass Guitars
Materials Hardwoods · Electronics

The Brief

Build custom instruments tailored to player specifications and aesthetic preferences.

The Process

From wood selection through body shaping, neck carving, finishing, and final assembly.

The Result

Professional-grade instruments combining playability with visual appeal.

Design

Body & Neck Design

Each build starts with carefully selected hardwoods—maple, mahogany, walnut—chosen for tonal properties and visual character. Body and neck profiles are designed for comfort and playability.

Fabrication

Body Shaping & Assembly

Body shaping combines power tools with hand work—router templates for consistency, hand planes and scrapers for contouring. Neck construction includes precise fret slot cutting and inlay work.

Finished Product

Completed Instruments

Each finished instrument undergoes complete setup—nut filing, fret leveling, intonation adjustment—ensuring professional playability and tone.
