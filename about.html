About

I'm Isaac Carson, a fabricator and mechanical designer with a focus on bringing ideas from concept to finished product. My work spans CNC machining, metal fabrication, woodworking, and CAD design.

What I Do

My projects typically start with a concept or client need, move through CAD modeling and design refinement, then into manufacturing using CNC mills, lathes, welding equipment, and hand tools. I work with a range of materials including steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and hardwoods.

Beyond individual projects, I handle the full process: design, CAM programming, machining, fabrication, finishing, and installation when needed.
